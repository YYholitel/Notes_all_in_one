- Identity Laws 同一律
		$p \land T \equiv p$ 
	- $p\lor F \equiv p$

- commutativ laws 交换律
	- $p \lor q \equiv q \lor p$ 
	- $p\land q \equiv q \lor p$
- donmintation laws 
	- $p\lor T\equiv T$
	- $p\land F \equiv F$
- Associative laws
	- $(p\lor q) \lor r \equiv p \lor (q \lor r)$
- Idependent laws 
	- $p \lor p \equiv p$
	- $p \land p \equiv p$
- Distributive laws 
	- $p \land(q\lor r)\equiv(p\land q)\lor(p \land r)$ 
  - De Morgan's Laws 德摩根律
  - 就是双否 
	  - $\lnot (p\lor q) \equiv \lnot p \land \lnot q$
	  - $\lnot(p \land q) \equiv \lnot p \lor \lnot q$
- contrapositive 
- Negation laws
	- $p \lor \lnot p \equiv T$
	- $p \land \lnot p \equiv F$
